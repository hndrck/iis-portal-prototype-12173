Troubleshooting

If the main app shows a blank page:

  • Check Console: Press F12 and look for red errors
  • Check Network: In F12 → Network tab, verify all JS/CSS files load (status 200)
  • Hard Refresh: Try Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
  • Clear Cache: Clear browser cache and reload
